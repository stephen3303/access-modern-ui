=== start | Getting Started
The Kit Demo shows the parts of the access-modern-ui kit working on fictional items. Each page in the sidebar shows a group of them.

# Getting help
Click **Help** in the sidebar, or press **F1**, to open help for the page you are on. Choose any other topic from the list on the left, or type in the search box to find the topics that mention a word. Press **Enter** in the search box to open the first match.

# Go anywhere with Ctrl+K
Press **Ctrl+K** and start typing the name of a page or of an item you opened lately. Press **Enter** to go to the first match, or use the **Up** and **Down** arrows to choose another. **Esc** closes it.

# Other things in the sidebar
- **Appearance** opens the list of color palettes. Click one to switch the whole app to it.
- **Folder trust** explains how to trust this folder, so Access stops asking to enable content.
- The **three lines** at the top fold the sidebar to icons, to give the page more room. Click them again to open it. Point at an icon to see its name.

=== cards | Cards and progress
This page shows cards, progress bars and the kit's message, toast and input dialogs.

# Try it
- Click **Primary** to see a message, and **Outline** to see a toast.
- Click **Ask** to answer a question in the kit's input dialog.
- Pick **All**, **Open** or **Done** above the table to filter it.

=== tables | Tables
This page shows two styled tables. Click a column caption to sort by it; click it again to reverse the order.

# Find items
- Click a **tab** or a **chip** to show only the items in that status. Each shows how many items it holds.
- Type in the **search box** to match an item or owner. Matches are marked in the table.
- Click the **funnel** beside a caption to show only some of that column's values.

=== tables.columns | Choosing columns
Click **Columns** at the top right of the first table to choose which columns it shows and in what order. Your choice is remembered for your Windows account.

=== list | List and detail
Click an item in the list to open it in the detail panel on the right. Change any field and click **Save**.

# Other actions
- The **More** button (three dots) on the detail card marks the item complete, flags it or deletes it. **Right-click** a row in the list for the same actions.
- **Print** and **Export** at the top of the list print the list or save it as a CSV file, in the order shown.

=== steps | Steps and sections
This page shows a step indicator and a set of sections that open and close.

# Try it
- Click **Next** and **Back** to move between the steps. Click a finished step's check mark to go back to it.
- Click a section's title to open it. Opening one closes the others.

=== design | Form designer
This page lays out a form by dragging. It places the kit's elements (cards, buttons, labels, progress bars, text, date and secret boxes, toggles, segmented controls, sliders, ratings, steppers, search boxes, stat tiles, alerts, avatars, empty states, tables, chips, tab strips, breadcrumbs and accordions), saves the layout, and builds a real form from it. On the canvas, elements other than cards, buttons, labels and progress bars show as an outlined box naming what they will be; Generate shows them for real.

# Try it
- Drag an element from **Elements** onto the canvas. Or click it, then click the canvas where it should go. **Esc** cancels.
- Drag an element to move it. Drag the small square on its bottom-right corner to resize it. Everything snaps to an 8px grid.
- Click an element to select it. **Double-click** it, or click **Edit** under **Selected** (or press **Enter**), for its settings: a card's title, a button's caption and style, a segmented control's options, a slider's range, a stepper's steps and so on. Settings the kit can't build are refused with the reason. Click **Delete** to remove it.
- With an element selected, the **arrow keys** move it 8px, or 1px with **Shift**. **Delete** removes it.

# Several at once
- **Shift-click** (or **Ctrl-click**) an element to add it to the selection, or take it out. Or drag a box over the empty canvas: every element wholly inside it is selected. **Ctrl+A** selects them all.
- Drag any selected element to move them all; the arrow keys and **Delete** work on them all too. A click on one of them leaves just that one selected.
- Click **Arrange** (the button beside **Delete**) to **Duplicate** the selection (**Ctrl+D**; copies land 16px down and right, and become the selection), **align** its left edges, centers, right edges, tops, middles or bottoms, or, with three or more, **distribute** them so the gaps between them are equal. A tab strip or breadcrumb isn't copied: a form takes one.
- **Undo** and **Redo** (the arrows left of **Form**, or **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Y**) step back and forward through the last 50 changes, with the selection as it was. Opening, importing or starting a layout starts them again.

# The form
- Click **Form** for what the layout builds. **Page or dialog**: a page fills the app's window; a dialog opens over it, with the **title** on its title bar. **Width** and **height** set its size, shown as the outlined area of the canvas: elements stay inside it. **Record source** is the table or query the form shows.
- With a record source, an element that holds a value (a text, date or secret box, a toggle, segmented control, slider, rating, progress bar, stat tile or avatar) can be bound to one of its fields: pick it under **Field** in the element's settings. The form shows the first record.
- On a page, **Anchor** in an element's settings keeps it at the window's right or bottom edge as the window resizes. A card, label, text box or button can also stretch across, or across and down.
- Click **Tab order** for the order the **Tab** key moves through the form. It is the reading order (top to bottom, then left to right) until you move an element with **Move up** or **Move down**; **Reading order** puts it back.
- A **table** lists a table or query of its own, picked under **Lists**: it needs an **id** field. Its **columns** are fields of it, separated by **;** (leave them empty for its first four fields), with optional captions. On a page it can stretch across, or across and down. Its rows take clicks; the Tab key skips it.
- **Chips**, a **tab strip** and a **breadcrumb** take their captions separated by **;**. A form takes one tab strip and one breadcrumb. An **accordion** takes its sections, a body height, and whether one or any number open at a time; its bodies are empty, for the app to fill.

# Saving
Type a name under **Layout name** and click **Save**. Pick a saved layout from **Open a layout** to show it again, and click **More** (the button with three dots), then **New layout**, for an empty canvas. If you leave with changes that aren't saved, you are asked whether to save them first.

# Layout files
**More**, then **Export layout**, saves the layout on the canvas as a **.json** file: to move it to another database, or keep it with an app's source. **Import layout** reads one back as a new layout (its name gets a number if a saved layout already has it); save it to keep it. Elements the kit can't build as the file has them (a kind this version doesn't know, settings out of range, a second tab strip) are left out, and a toast says how many. A record source or field this database lacks stays in the layout: Generate says what to change.

# Generate
Click **Generate** to build the layout as a real form and show it in a window of its own. It saves the layout first if it has changes. It also writes the form's build code to the **designer** folder beside the database: **Build_pg<Name>.txt** holds the calls that build the form, ready to add to an app's build, and **pg<Name>.vba** (or **dlg<Name>.vba** for a dialog) is its code, with an empty handler for each button, and for each toggle, segmented control, slider, rating, stepper, search box, chip row, tab strip and breadcrumb, a GridClick for the tables, and a Form_Load that gives each table its rows. A table's rows are a form of their own, built first. In the preview, chips and tabs light up when clicked. A page's preview can be resized to try its anchors. A bound preview shows the first record but can't change it. The message after Generate counts the form's controls; past 500 it warns, since Access allows 754 controls in a form's life. Generate needs the editable .accdb in full Access: a locked copy can't build forms.
