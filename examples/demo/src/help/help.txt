rebuild the front end after editing it. Everything above the first topic is ignored.

Format:
  === key | Title      starts a topic. A key with a dot (tables.columns) is listed
                       indented under its parent. Page keys match frmMain's page keys.
  # Heading            a heading inside the topic
  - text               a bullet point

  Other lines are paragraph text; a blank line ends a paragraph.

=== start | Getting Started
The Kit Demo shows the parts of the access-modern-ui kit working on fictional items. Each page in the sidebar shows a group of them.

# Getting help
Click **Help** in the sidebar, or press **F1**, to open help for the page you are on. Choose any other topic from the list on the left, or type in the search box to find the topics that mention a word. Press **Enter** in the search box to open the first match.

# Other things in the sidebar
- **Appearance** opens the list of color palettes. Click one to switch the whole app to it.
- **Folder trust** explains how to trust this folder, so Access stops asking to enable content.
- **Ask AI** opens a chat window for an AI service. Its **Settings** choose the service and where the API key comes from; nothing works until a key is set.
- **Classification** chooses the app's classification. The colored banners at the top and bottom of the window show it, and the app only permits classification labels at or below it. Choosing **SECRET**, for example, permits UNCLASSIFIED, CUI, CONFIDENTIAL and SECRET labels, but not TOP SECRET.

=== cards | Cards and progress
This page shows cards, progress bars and the kit's message, toast and input dialogs.

# Try it
- Click **Primary** to see a message, and **Outline** to see a toast.
- Click **Ask** to answer a question in the kit's input dialog.
- Pick **All**, **Open** or **Done** above the table to filter it.

=== tables | Tables
This page shows two styled tables. Click a column caption to sort by it; click it again to reverse the order.

# Find items
- Click a **tab** or a **chip** to show only the items in that status. Each shows how many items it holds.
- Type in the **search box** to match an item or owner. Matches are marked in the table.
- Click the **funnel** beside a caption to show only some of that column's values.

=== tables.columns | Choosing columns
Click **Columns** at the top right of the first table to choose which columns it shows and in what order. Your choice is remembered for your Windows account.

=== list | List and detail
Click an item in the list to open it in the detail panel on the right. Change any field and click **Save**.

# Other actions
- The **More** button (three dots) on the detail card marks the item complete, flags it or deletes it.
- **Print** and **Export** at the top of the list print the list or save it as a CSV file, in the order shown.

=== steps | Steps and sections
This page shows a step indicator and a set of sections that open and close.

# Try it
- Click **Next** and **Back** to move between the steps. Click a finished step's check mark to go back to it.
- Click a section's title to open it. Opening one closes the others.

=== ai | Ask AI
Type a question or request and press **Send** (or **Enter**; **Ctrl+Enter** starts a new line). The reply is added to the conversation above, and your next message follows on from it.

# Good to know
- **New chat** starts again, with nothing remembered.
- **Copy reply** puts the last reply on the clipboard.
- **Cancel** (Send while it waits) stops waiting for a reply.
- **Settings** chooses the service, model and where the API key comes from.
- What you type is sent to the AI service. Send only what that service is approved for.

=== ai.settings | AI settings
- **Kind of service** picks the way the app talks to the service: OpenAI or a compatible one (Azure OpenAI, Ollama, LM Studio and others), Anthropic, or Google Gemini. Picking one fills in its usual address, model and key variable.
- **Model** is the model name the service knows. **Service address** is where requests go; {model} in it is replaced by the model name.
- **API key** is never saved by the app. Name an **environment variable** that holds it, or a **file** whose first line is the key, or type a key for **this session only** (forgotten when Access closes). A service on this computer needs no key.
- **Key header** is only for services that want the key in a different header, such as api-key for Azure OpenAI.
- **Standing instructions** are sent with every message, for example "Answer in two sentences."
- **Longest reply** limits the reply length in tokens. 0 leaves the limit out, which some models need.
- **Test** saves and sends a short message to check the settings.

=== ai.connect | Connect to AI
Connects parts of a form to the AI without writing code: choose a form that is open, the **button** that asks, the **box with the question**, and the **box for the answer**. Leave the answer box empty to see the answer in a message.

- An **instruction** tells the AI what to do with the text, for example "Summarize this note in one sentence."
- **Connect** works at once on an open form, and the connection is applied each time the form opens.
- Connecting a button that already does something replaces that until the form is opened again, so choose a button made for this.
- **Disconnect** forgets the connection; the button stops asking the AI the next time the form opens.
